# Ring Toss King — CrazyGames Submission Kit

Prepared: 2026-11-17. All copy in English. Game is HTML5 / Canvas, no external assets.

## 1. Game Description (paste into the Description field)

Step right up to **Ring Toss King**, a cozy night-market ring toss game. Fling rings at a row of prizes — bears, ducks, soda bottles, gift boxes and the shiny gold egg. The farther and smaller the prize, the more points you score. Chain hits together to build a combo and double your points.

Aim by dragging up, then release to toss. The arc preview shows where your ring will land, but a little wobble keeps every throw exciting. Land three or more in a row to trigger a 2x combo multiplier and chase your high score.

- Simple one-finger controls — easy to pick up, hard to master
- Three rows of prizes with increasing difficulty and rewards
- Combo system, live score and a saved personal best
- Warm night-market atmosphere with lanterns and string lights
- Crisp canvas graphics and synthesized sound effects — no downloads, plays instantly in your browser

How far can your aim take you? Toss a few rings and find out.
